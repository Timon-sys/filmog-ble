The fogger has no watchdog of its own: it fogs until it hears "smoke off", even if the
    Bluetooth link drops. This page sends stop on release, blur, tab switch, close and reconnect, but if the PC
    dies mid-burst the fogger keeps going, so stay near the battery. Turn the real remote off first: the fogger
    takes one connection at a time.


  

    
    
This removes the safe limits. You can send any fog power (0 to 100000 on the slider, any number in the
      field), any fan value from 0 to 100, and hold or burst for up to 60 seconds. The green bands under the
      sliders show the safe range. The pump guard then only warns instead of stopping the smoke.

    
The FilMog is a heated coil with an air pump. With the fan at 0 the coil heats with no airflow and the
      wick cooks; you can hear it crackle. Power below 8000 makes no smoke, and nothing above power 40000 has
      been tested. You use this at your own risk.
